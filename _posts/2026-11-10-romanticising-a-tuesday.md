There is a version of me that only drinks coffee out of the nice cup. I am trying to be her more often. Today she put on a record, opened the window, and watched the light do its thing across the floor like it was a film she'd directed.

It reminded me of being twenty and thinking every ordinary afternoon was a scene. Maybe it was. Maybe it still is.

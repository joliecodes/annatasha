This year and last year have been intense, dramatic, and extremely emotionally overstimulating. So many things happened adjacent to me—yet it ended up forcing me to really, really reflect on things to do specifically about me.

I officially can say, with all my chest, I don’t know who I am. 

I’m not trying to stroke my own dick when I say everyone who gets to know me, and finds out one by one what I can do, some of which I am professionally certified to do, they always say, “You can do everything!” Or “Is there anything you cannot do?”

The irony is that the initial question, the one that starts all new connections, “So what do you do?”, I end up feeling a bit embarrassed to answer it, because in all honesty, I don’t do anything. There is not one thing I can call my own, as there is not one thing I can claim I’ve mastered. And that in it’s own makes me feel, oddly, embarrassed.

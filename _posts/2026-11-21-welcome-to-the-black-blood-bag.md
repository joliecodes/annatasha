Good morning from the hospital bed and hello from the other side of the My Chemical Romance saga. 

Initially I had a whole "comeback" blog entry, one that covers the excruciatingly overstimulating year and a half I've had. A lot of reflections, you know, that type of in-depth insight into where my mind has been and how I've been doing.

But then the MCR concert crept up and I thought, okay, the concert might bring up some other things in me that will be worth noting in my "comeback" entry. And boy, the concert brought something up, alright. Blood. Yes. BLOOD. Actual, literal BLOOD. Are y'all ready? This is a wild one. 

Maybe I should put this:

**TW: Mentions of blood, haemorrhage, medical surprises and close calls.**

I have been looking forward to the MCR concert since the moment I scored tickets last year, my good friend Justin in tow watching my heart rate peak at 110bpm just waiting for the clock to countdown. As all of you know, after a postponement and a lot of worry that that postponement actually meant a cancellation, show day arrived.

I had told Nazeera (I call her Adik) that my period was due a day before the concert, and joked that I bid everyone around me good luck because they'd have to deal with a hormonal hot mess as I cried my lungs out, shouting back the lyrics Gerard Way sang to me via my iPod earphones back at him. Just good emo jokes all round right up till Thursday morning. A big chunk of this emo train of jokes was that I had therapy scheduled for 12.00pm on the day of the show--perfect for regulating so I can completely, unapologetically "dysregulate" at 8.30pm that night when MCR comes on.

My period actually came Thursday morning, and I was alright. In my mind I thought, _hey, it might be tougher and tiring to get through the concert today, but it's just my period._ I did a whole beat (full-face makeup for anyone who didn't know what that meant, lol) in the morning, went to therapy, had a great session, refilled my meds, came home and felt my cramps creep up. 

In the past couple of cycles, I've found myself actually using a heat-pack to help manage my cramps, which has never been a thing to me before this year. So, naturally, I did all I could to manage the pain before one of the biggest concert nights in my life. Took Panadol extra, heat-pack, lay down. My friends, Adik, Prita and Darsh arrived to carpool to the concert, and my sweet Prita kept asking me if I was feeling better. I told her, "Yes! Maybe just so anxious because I'm so excited!"

And honestly, the period cramps came and went, as they usually do on day 1 of my cycle. 

Fastforward to when the janitor came out to sweep the floor before you hear the heart monitor beeping (_you already know what it is!!!_) and I'm already crying. Full-sobbing before Gerard even starts with, "Well, come one, come all..." I had been waiting for this exact moment for so long. And I must admit, I knew I was going to scream my lungs out to The End. as though I was the most hurt person in the crowd.

It felt _so satisfying_ to scream, "When I grow up... I want to be... NOTHING AT ALL," and I might have scared the sweet couple behind me a little bit, but I settled down a little cos I felt something tight in my chest. I thought... I am not going to pass out during song 1. This is not how I will spend the night. Dead! was played and I told Ryan and Adik that I just want to stay for This Is How I Disappear and then I know I need water; I'm not trying to make my uterus angrier, the pain was coming and going again, and more obviously now.

> I felt something tight in my chest. I thought... I am not going to pass out during song 1. 

All I wanted to scream was the bridge of This Is How I Disappear, and the moment that song ended, I turned to Ryan and Adik and said, "Okay! I need water! I'm heading out that way!" To my surprise, there were free hydration stations, and I was like "Oh GREAT!" because I thought I'd have to walk to the top of the stadium for some hydration. And to be frank, the view from the side of the stage was way clearer. We decided we'd hang around there--closer to the water too. I was SO thirsty.

I danced and headbanged so much, even threw ass during Mama. No one could stop me. But something eventually did. 

My cramps hit me again but this time it didn't go away. I had to unbutton my jeans because I felt so bloated and the button + zip were adding some pressure to my womb area. Just as this was happening, the big fire bursts lit up the stage and I swore I was going to pass out from the heat. I told Ryan we should move away from the fire. A marshall saw me struggle and asked me if I would like to go to the medic. I declined at first, but very soon after asked Ryan to ask them if they had panadol.

Of course they did, I sat with the medics, drank more water, consumed some Panadol while Disenchanted was playing and knowing Sleep was not far away, I went back to join the crowd again, and promised the medic I'll sit down and watch the rest of the show. He offered to wheel me out and let me watch on a wheelchair but I declined, saying "tak rugged la camtu", persistent on being fucking metal till the end of the show.

Ryan became my armchair for Sleep (I sat against him to watch, my cramps still hurting, consistently), and I couldn't even sing. If I sang, I felt like passing out. I just had to mouth the words. I couldn't even see the screen anymore. I just stared at the sky and the blues that shone against the stadium ceiling--full cinematic jiwang experience type shit.

And then I felt really weak. I suggested we go to the food vendors and get some sugary drinks, so we trekked our way up to the stalls and I remembered from The Weeknd concert, there was a seating area right behind the stalls, overlooking the stage, that we could set up base to reduce the audio stimulation too. 

When I got up there, I immediately lay down in fetal position. First on two foldable chairs, and then I moved to the concrete floor, which was nice cos it was cool to the touch. Ryan came back with some Ice Lemon Tea, I sipped it and would try to remain lying down. Until Our Lady Of Sorrows came on and I shot up (hurt myself in the process--worth it), sang that, and lay back down.

As they started the second part of Demolition Lovers (remember when it was 2 different tracks on your winamp?) I told Ryan I needed to be back at least in the back of the pit to watch it. 

Song ended, lights came on, I was shocked that they ended it on that song (I loved it though I had hoped for The Ghost Of You to close off the night), and we started making our way out. 

We found Adik at the end of the tunnel and started on our journey to the Sri Petaling LRT station, because I had a hunch that there would be no queue to get onto the train and that would be better even though we'd have to walk further (what is it? Like 2km from the stadium?) I was singing all the songs from the B Set, not to be obnoxious (or maybe a little) but more because it helped take my mind of the pain in my uterus. Every step I took, the impact of a footstep made it ache a little bit. In my head I'm thinking, _you're almost home. Maggi Tom Yum Kaw and heat pack, a shower, and this will be the perfect night, despite the fact your uterus is abusing you right now._

Ryan even offered to piggy-back ride me, but I felt that the pressure of my tummy against his back might hurt me more.

We got back to Bandar Tasik Selatan LRT station and skipped right to KK Mart and I bought: Panadol Extra, 100 Plus, Tiger Balm heating lotion. Got into the car, consumed and used my "old woman" products, sat back and enjoyed more MCR songs on the way home.

Once I got home, I went straight for the heat pack but suddenly I was hurting enough to not want to eat anymore (this is bamboozling, I always want my Maggi, and me being too tired/in pain for a bowl? Should have known something was up.)

Next morning: I couldn't make it out of bed to send Jolie to school. Ryan did the school run, came back, and asked me what did I want to do. I said I just need to rest. I do remember telling Ryan, "I think I injured myself. This feels like an injury." But eventually I thought, okay, I need to get up, I have soundcheck for our new band's first test gig at 7.30pm. It's 3 songs. I just need to make it through 3 songs.

But I could not stand up straight. I called my mum to ask if Kak Mira (Mum 2.0) can come and accompany me when Jolie is home, because I needed to nurse the cramps properly before the gig. A lot of back and forth in the family group chat and next thing I know, Jolie and I are filing into my mum's car. 

**_I'm going to put a ☘︎ next to every important thing to remember, because it matters in this story._**

"☘︎ GP or Hospital?" my mum asked. 

I thought about it for a while, and thought, hmm, "Hospital. They'll give the stronger stuff to fix me up for my gig."

Straight to Emergency Department and SURPRISINGLY, the wait wasn't long. I was so happy because it meant I would have time to go home and put on my makeup for the show. 

But then the Emergency doctor that attended to me asked me if I wanted to do a CT scan ☘︎. I'm sus of every big machine test because why? _These are just period pains?_ I asked him, "Ultrasound cannot ah?" and the doctor said because I just ate, the ultrasound might not give a very clear picture. 

I said alright then let's do the CT scan. "But can I make it in time for my show at 7.30?" 

Doctor said, "4.30-ish we should have the results back. I'm just worried it might be your appendix." 

I just shrugged and followed instructions. They actually managed to slit me into a slot right before another patient's LONG CT scan (since mine would take only 10 minutes), and the doctor told me he'll get someone to administer painkillers in the mean time. The nurse came to get me and I asked her how long can I go home for before I come back for the results?

The sweet lady looked at me, confused, and said, "Go home? No la, you stay here. So we can observe you. The painkiller strong oh, scared you dizzy or vomit." I sighed and climbed into the Emergency Ward bed. Painkillers administered, family came in and out of the emergency ward in twos to plan Jolie's care for the rest of the evening leading up to the gig.

I texted Isaac, "If it's not appendicitis, I'm playing the gig."

"It's appendicitis," the doctor announced as he came through my curtain. 

_Ah, fuck_. "So have to do surgery today? Usually it's like that right?" 

Doctor, "Yeah, but wait let me get the General Surgeon for you." 

Dr. George walks in and tells me, "You've been diagnosed with appendicitis, but it's so mild. However your infection marker from your blood work is very high. Too high for this early level appendicitis."

"Oh no. So something else is wrong?"

"Yes, but since the report diagnosed you with appendicitis, we can treat that first. I'm thinking I need to check your intestines/colon. Maybe the infection is there."

"Could you do a colonoscopy while I'm under? While you take out my appendix?" 

"I could honestly treat all of it with antibiotics ☘︎, but since the appendix will eventually need to be removed anyways, we might as well take it out and find the source of the infection. Cos it's definitely not just the appendix."

I immediately texted Isaac that I'm getting my appendix + colonoscopy removed THAT NIGHT and I won't be able to play the gig. 

Fast forward to surgery, 9.00pm. In recent times I've been put under, no one's babied me haha. The anaesthetist will just say, "Okay! See ya!"

I think I even said, "See ya in an hour!" because that's how long I was told the procedure would be.

I woke up, and I don't know why, but every time I've come out of GA, my first question has always been, "Did I do a good job?" 

USUALLY, I'd get some laughs. But this time the observation nurse said, "Ok puan, Doctor will see you later okay!"

Immediately, even in a haze, I was confused. "What time is it?"

"11.30pm, puan," she answered, and if i could open my eyes, it'd shoot open wide.

"Kenapa lama sangat? Apa jadi?" / "Why did it take so long? What happened?" 

"Doctor will explain later, puan." 

I was getting anxious and more confused. I got wheeled back to my room and saw Ryan looking panicked. I might be wrong but I think I heard him ask the nurse, "Is she awake?" and when I said something, he said my mum was freaking out. 

As they locked my bed back into position, the nurse said, "Please be cautions of your draining tube okay?" and she lifted my gown to show me a tube sticking out of a hole in my stomach.

"WHY DO I HAVE A TUBE?" I feel like I almost shouted it, but I was pretty groggy still. But I got really freaked out. I thought this was an appendix removal. I think I immediately grabbed my phone and texted what I call The Sisters Groupchat (my groupchat with The Impatient Sisters) because Kina had her appendix removed last year. I asked her if she had a drainage tube. She said... uh no.

I'm getting frustrated. I'm low key scared.

I hear Dr. George come in, "Your ovarian cyst ruptured. There was a lot of blood."

"I had an ovarian cyst?!" was all I could manage.

He explained a bit more to me, but our proper follow up was when I finally learned the whole picture: 

Yes, we went in for an appendicectomy. I've seen it on Grey's Anatomy, yeah, I know it's simple. No tube is usually needed lol. But first task on the list was to do the colonoscopy first. Dr. said he just saw a little bit of inflammation in my colon, nothing concerning, took a sample for biopsy anyways, and then went on to get my appendix.

When he went in with the scope, all he saw was blood. He didn't know where the blood was coming from, but, in his words, my appendix was swimming in blood. He said it was a lot of blood. it was all around my body cavity, up to my lungs. He had to drain A LOT of blood to find the source of the bleeding. MY ORGANS were marinating in BLOOD. 

The source was the ruptured ovarian cyst on my left ovary that cause a haemorrhage. He said it was big. He had to cauterise it to stop the bleeding, drain all that internal bleeding, then he took out my appendix. I was under close observation the whole of the first night because the fear was that my main blood stream would get infected too. 

The gynaecologist and Dr. George both agreed that the ovarian cyst ruptured at the concert. And that I had been internally bleeding probably since I was at the concert, so maybe about 24 hours I was collecting blood in my body from the haemorrhage, all whilst calling it "period pains". I mean, okay, it's pretty scary. Cos by this timeline, with yucky cyst-y juice mixed with haemorrhage blood, I could've gone into sepsis.

And to think:

☘︎ almost just went to a GP
☘︎ almost just did an ultrasound instead of a CT scan
☘︎ almost delayed the appendicectomy (without going in for my appendix, we would have never found the internal bleeding)

I'm still at the hospital and have to check my blood work tomorrow to make damn sure I'm clear of any infection. My biggest frustration is the drainage tube coming out of me, I cannot wait to get this out!!!

A lot of my friends are asking how I'm telling this story with "lols" and "hehe" but honestly, I had no time to fear for my life. I was put to sleep thinking I was doing a standard procedure + a quick test, but woke up finding out I just had my life saved. This is Grey's Anatomy level type script okay. And truthfully, I don't know how else I would be able to tell the story! It makes me a little nervous and so I laugh. 

I hope to get the tube out real soon, and continue my recovery. I've had to cancel some plans (I cried like a baby when the doctor said I should cancel my trip with Jolie) and I reckon I will need to take it easy when I do eventually get to go home. 

Let me just say, I also cackle thinking I bled for My Chemical Romance, internally and literally, and saying things like, "I would die for My Chemical Romance" from this moment on will actually hold real cold hard evidence haha. HARDCORE!

But in all seriousness, please don't downplay any pain. Please go get checked no matter how severe. Period pains are painful but not meant to be ignored, even if it's normal to you. Go to the doctor. Always, ALWAYS go to a doctor. 

It's wild cos I haven't even said it but, I almost died but I got really lucky that all the forks and choices led me here, ALIVE. Feels good to write again! (Also feels good to be alive cos means I can write again haha--ok ok no more jokes.)
